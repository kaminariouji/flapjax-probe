Conclusion. The reconciliation the receipt asks for holds to the wei: the balance just after the 10-11 buy, minus every BNB outflow in the window (value and gas), equals the balance just before the 10-12 buy exactly (no BNB in). Every transaction is a bounty payout (FLAPJAX) or a gas grant (0.002 BNB to the escrow address). The one wrong item is the line itself: "0.000025 fees" should have read 0.0020265546 (value + fees) — or at minimum, the fees figure 0.0000265546 — and the 0.002 gas grant should have been itemised. Stated as a gap: 0.0020015546 BNB unaccounted on the receipt line (0.002 grant + 0.0000015546 fee understatement).
